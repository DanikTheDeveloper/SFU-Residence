## Distinctive building treatment — instructions, not invented facade facts
- Modern towers (East, West, Courtyard): unique building mass/wing outlines, dense repeated facade modules and actual story proportions; glass/opaque wall ratio should come from legal photo references; no generic blank boxes.
- North Towers: 3 separately positioned masses, facades/roofline consistent with photos and different signage/name metadata. If they are nearly identical physically, reuse a prefab with per-building material/sign variation; avoid three anonymous rectangles.
- MCH / Hamilton: low-rise multi-section building volumes; proper footprint and roof profile, meaningful windows and entrance features.
- Shell House and Dining Commons: place and size by geo data; verify how many stories and facade details rather than inventing hard-coded floors.
- Nine townhouses: actual nine separately named buildings in the western housing area, their own footprints / orientation / gabled or flat roof **based on confirmed references**, recurring siding/windows/stairs. Do NOT place the nine in an arbitrarily straight line if independent geometry says otherwise.

## Register completion criterion
For each row: unique scene node or data entry, consistent building ID and readable inspection/sign label, footprint origin and geometry, height strategy, collision, visual type, reference/provenance, confidence. Never conflate building names in labels.
