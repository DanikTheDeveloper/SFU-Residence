## Findings
- The page explicitly labels the top as facing the north parking lot, allowing facade orientation to be inferred rather than guessed from a cropped photo.
- All three show a central courtyard lounge projection, with five room bays on either side in the central inner facade. Earlier end-lounge modeling was a misreading of cropped courtyard photographs and is superseded.
- Barbara Rae and Shadbolt have the more projecting end stair on the west arm. Pauline Jewett's equivalent projecting stair is on the east arm. Shared master now retains that exception.
- Neither public housing PDF includes level1/ground floor. The columned undercroft and closed lounge base come from exterior photographs. Their dimensions and rear exit layout remain estimated.
- The current SFU housing webpage says seven floors, but BRH/PJH plans extend through floor8. Shadbolt PDF stops at7. Facilities lists PJH eight levels, agreeing with the preserved registry and earlier insurance evidence. No floor-count or roof-height change made.
- Facilities' full key-plan link redirects to SFU CAS authentication. No access-control bypass attempted; public references are sufficient to continue exterior work.

## Limits

Exact stair-screen handedness is inferred from plan topology plus user photos, not separately verified by identified photos of every tower.
